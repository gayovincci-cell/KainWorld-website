Popular Filipino Foods

Explore our most loved dishes, from classic favorites to delicious Filipino specialties.

🇵🇭 Classic Favorite

Chicken Adobo

A classic Filipino dish of tender chicken simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and spices.

🥘 Ingredients

  • Chicken — 1 kg
  • Soy sauce — ½ cup (120 ml)
  • Vinegar — ½ cup (120 ml)
  • Garlic — 6 cloves
  • Bay leaves — 3 pieces
  • Whole peppercorns — 1 tsp
Regional Favorite

Sinigang

A tangy and savory Filipino soup made with meat, vegetables and tamarind.

🥘 Ingredients

  • Pork — 500 g
  • Tamarind — 50 g
  • Tomatoes — 2 pieces
  • Onion — 1 piece
  • Radish — 1 piece
  • String beans — 100 g
Street Favorite

Lumpia

Crispy Filipino spring rolls filled with seasoned meat and vegetables.

🥘 Ingredients

  • Ground pork — 500 g
  • Lumpia wrappers — 25 pieces
  • Carrots — 1 medium
  • Onion — 1 medium
  • Garlic — 3 cloves
  • Salt — 1 tsp
Fiesta Favorite

Lechon

A famous Filipino roasted pork dish known for its crispy skin and flavorful meat.

🥘 Ingredients

  • Whole pig — 1 (6–8 kg)
  • Garlic — 20 cloves
  • Lemongrass — 5 stalks
  • Salt — 2 tbsp
  • Black pepper — 1 tbsp
  • Bay leaves — 10 pieces
Pampanga Favorite

Sisig

A savory Filipino dish made with chopped pork, onions, chili and calamansi.

🥘 Ingredients

  • Pork — 500 g
  • Onion — 1 medium
  • Calamansi juice — 3 tbsp
  • Chili peppers — 2 pieces
  • Soy sauce — 2 tbsp
  • Mayonnaise — 2 tbsp
Dessert Favorite

Halo-Halo

A colorful Filipino dessert made with shaved ice, milk, fruits and sweet toppings.

🍨 Ingredients

  • Shaved ice — 2 cups
  • Sweet beans — ½ cup
  • Ube — ½ cup
  • Mixed fruits — 1 cup
  • Evaporated milk — ½ cup
  • Leche flan — 1 piece
Filipino Classic

Kare-Kare

A rich Filipino stew made with tender beef, vegetables and a flavorful peanut sauce.

🥜 Ingredients

  • Beef - 1 kg
  • Peanut butter - % cup
  • Eggplant - 2 peaces
  • String beans - 200 g
  • Bok choy - 3 pieces
  • Ground rice - % cup
Grilled Favorite

Chicken Inasal

A popular Filipino grilled chicken marinated with vinegar, calamansi, garlic and spices.

🍗 Ingredients

  • Chicken - 1 kg
  • Calamansi Juice - % cup
  • Vinegar - % cup
  • Garlic - 6 cloves
  • Annatto oil - % cup
  • Brown sugar - 2 tbsp
Filipino Soup

Bulalo

A comforting Filipino beef soup made with tender beef shank, bone marrow, and vegetables.

🍲 Ingredients

  • Beef shank — 1 kg
  • Water — 8 cups
  • Corn — 2 pieces
  • Potatoes — 2 pieces
  • Cabbage — ½ head
  • Fish sauce — 2 tbsp
Dessert Favorite

Leche Flan

A smooth Filipino custard dessert made with egg yolks, condensed milk, and caramelized sugar.

🍮 Ingredients

  • Egg yolks — 10 pieces
  • Condensed milk — 1 can (390g)
  • Evaporated milk — 1 can (370ml)
  • Sugar — 1 cup
  • Vanilla extract — 1 teaspoon
